## Brand voice checklist (run before each post)
- [ ] Palette only paper / ink / cobalt / terracotta (+ moss as accent). No neon, glow, glass, blur or generic gradient anywhere, including covers.
- [ ] Fonts: Instrument Sans, Instrument Serif italic for one emphasized word, JetBrains Mono for small labels. Hairlines and hard offset shadows.
- [ ] Hook is clear in the first two seconds or the first slide, and a person could repeat it.
- [ ] One idea per post and one clear call to action (link in bio, DM "SITE", save, or comment).
- [ ] Caption is short, plain English, confident with a light wink. No "stunning", "game-changing", "unlock", "revolutionary", "award-winning", "high-converting". No hype or exclamation chains.
- [ ] Every claim is true and in the brief: 4–6 weeks, reply within one business day, Next.js, SEO and analytics from day one. No numbers about results, rankings, clients or prices.
- [ ] The featured design is a real slug from `src/data/prompts.json`, shown live, with the prompt mentioned. No mock client work or invented testimonials.
- [ ] Offer posts: "70% off the build and care, until November 30", no fake scarcity, no price until Ido sets it, CTA is DM "SITE".
- [ ] Hashtags: 8 to 12, the same base set as week 1 plus 2 specific to the post.
- [ ] Accessibility: alt text, readable contrast, captions on reels (silent by default), text inside the safe area.
- [ ] Ido has approved this post. Nothing goes live before that.
